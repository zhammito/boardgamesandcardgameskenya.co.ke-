Why we exist

Good games were hard to find here

Walk into most shops in town and you'll find one dusty Monopoly and a deck of playing cards. We wanted the whole shelf: every version of UNO, Skip-Bo, Scrabble for the kids, the loud party decks and the couples' games people usually order from abroad and wait weeks for.

So we stocked them, in Nairobi, and made them easy to buy. You pay with M-Pesa or card like you would for anything else. If you're in Nairobi, it can reach you the same day. If you're in Eldoret or Mombasa, we send it out to you.

We also made our own. The Kenya at 50 edition is a board game packed with over 4,000 Kenyan words, for 4 to 20 players, built for the kind of family gathering where everyone has an opinion.
